Privacy — FIVE by Droxion

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This preview game does not require an account. Game results, settings, statistics, and streak information are stored in the browser's local storage on your own device.

Data stored on your device

Word guesses, daily game status, practice game progress, game settings, and locally calculated statistics are stored by your browser. You can clear them by deleting this website's stored site data in your browser settings.

Advertising and payments

There are no ad integrations, payment collection, or premium subscriptions in this preview. If that changes, we will update this policy before enabling those services.

Hosting

Our hosting provider may process routine technical request data such as IP addresses and browser information for service delivery, security, and server logs. With optional permission, this website loads Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-J5WYY9ZFFV) to measure visits and gameplay events such as game starts, completions, practice selection, hints, and result sharing. If you decline, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. If you accept, Google may receive technical browser and device information, IP address for processing, page URLs, approximate location and usage events. We do not send your guessed letters, hidden answers, user-entered names, email addresses or full local game statistics to Google Analytics. You can revisit your decision using the Analytics choices link in the game footer. The choice is stored on your device; see Google's privacy documentation for information about Google's processing and available choices.

Sharing

If you choose to share a result, your device's sharing or clipboard function is used. The result includes a colored grid and score, not the secret word.

Changes

We may update this policy when the product adds features or services. Review this page for changes.
