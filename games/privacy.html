Privacy — FIVE by Droxion

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This preview game does not require an account. Game results, settings, statistics, and streak information are stored in the browser's local storage on your own device.

Data stored on your device

Word guesses, daily game status, practice game progress, game settings, and locally calculated statistics are stored by your browser. You can clear them by deleting this website's stored site data in your browser settings.

Advertising and payments

There are no ad integrations, payment collection, or premium subscriptions in this preview. If that changes, we will update this policy before enabling those services.

Hosting

Our hosting provider may process routine technical request data such as IP addresses and browser information for service delivery, security, and server logs. This website does not currently run third-party marketing analytics.

Sharing

If you choose to share a result, your device's sharing or clipboard function is used. The result includes a colored grid and score, not the secret word.

Changes

We may update this policy when the product adds features or services. Review this page for changes.
