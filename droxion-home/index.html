Learn to play

Three ways to make better guesses

Start with varied letters

Early guesses such as CRANE or STARE can test common letters. Avoid repeating letters in your first guess unless you have a good reason.

Use every clue

Green tiles stay in place; yellow tiles belong somewhere else. Remember that repeated letters are counted carefully.

Slow down near the end

Before guessing, compare your candidate with every prior clue. A good final guess follows all the information you have.
