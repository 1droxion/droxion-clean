Turn your gameplay into five highlight clips.

Try the Windows creator studio: turn recordings into five vertical MP4 highlight candidates, apply original styles and add your own captions. Online video processing and subscriptions aren't available yet.

Open Online Editor (Private Beta) ↗ Download Windows beta ↗

The online editor is a private, password-protected beta. Upload your own recording (up to 120 MB and 10 minutes), choose a style, captions and font, and pick any of five clips to download. YouTube links require your original footage. No payments are collected.

Made for OBS creators, too.

The Windows beta can monitor your local OBS recordings folder and produce five highlight candidates when OBS saves a new completed recording or replay.

Have a YouTube video or livestream? The beta recognizes your own video link as a reference, but does not download streams or videos from YouTube. Use your original recording.

How It Works

  1. Upload gameplay you own or have permission to use.
  2. Choose a style and optional manual captions.
  3. Review and download five vertical MP4s selected using audio and motion.

Clip selection uses activity in the footage. Audience growth and earnings vary by creator and platform.
