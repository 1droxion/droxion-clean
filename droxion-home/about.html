About Droxion

Small games. Big moments of focus.

Droxion develops digital experiences, including FIVE, an independent daily five-letter word puzzle. We believe a good game should be clear, fast to learn, and enjoyable whether you play for a minute or stay for a challenge.

Why we made FIVE

Word puzzles are a simple way to practice deduction. You make a guess, get feedback, and improve the next guess. FIVE combines that familiar idea with daily challenges, unlimited practice, local progress, and straightforward controls.

What makes it different?

Our game offers a daily puzzle and a separate practice mode. A broad dictionary accepts common five-letter guesses. Optional hints are available after several attempts. Wins, streaks, and settings are stored in the player's browser, not tied to an account.

Independent game

FIVE by Droxion is an original independent game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The New York Times or Wordle.
