How to play FIVE

FIVE is a five-letter word-guessing puzzle. The goal is to discover the secret word in six tries or fewer. Every completed guess returns information that helps you narrow your options.

Step 1: Enter a five-letter word

Use your phone's on-screen keyboard or a physical keyboard. Enter any five-letter word accepted by the game, then press Enter. A short, common word is often a useful first guess because it reveals information about frequently used letters.

Example: Try CRANE as an opening guess. You're testing C, R, A, N and E in one attempt.

Step 2: Read the colored tiles

  • Green means the letter is correct and already in the correct position.
  • Yellow means that letter appears in the answer, but not in that position.
  • Gray means the letter does not belong in the answer, or its occurrences have already been accounted for in the feedback.

Step 3: Build your next guess from the clues

Suppose your guess reveals a green R in the second position and a yellow A in the third. Keep R in the second position, and try A somewhere else. Look for a five-letter word that also respects the letters already ruled out.

Repeated letters can be tricky

The same letter can appear more than once in a word. Imagine the answer APPLE and the guess ALLEY. The first A is green; the first L is yellow because an L appears later in APPLE. The second L is gray because there is only one L in the answer. The E is yellow because it belongs at the end.

Daily puzzle vs practice

The daily puzzle is the same for everyone on that UTC date. Once finished, your result is saved on your device. Practice mode generates more rounds without changing your daily winning streak.

What if you get stuck?

After three guesses, you may request one hint. FIVE reveals the correct letter for one position. When you share an assisted result, the game marks that hint was used.

Frequently asked questions

Is FIVE free?

Yes, the current version is free. There are no premium purchases or ads in the current game.

Can I play more than once?

You can play the daily puzzle once, but practice rounds are unlimited.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your progress and settings are saved in the browser on your own device.

Why don't I see yesterday's streak on another device?

The game does not currently synchronize progress across devices. Clearing your browser's site data may remove stored progress.

When is the next puzzle?

The next shared answer begins at 00:00 UTC. A countdown appears on the game page.
